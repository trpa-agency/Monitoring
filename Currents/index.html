Model hours in the archive. Speeds in mph. CSV

Each streak is a particle of surface water carried by the modeled current. Particles move at the model's real speed for the playback rate shown, so their trails show how far water travels. Brighter streaks are faster.

  • Currents and temperature: UC Davis Tahoe Environmental Research Center (TERC) three-dimensional lake model, published every two hours on TERC's Forecasted Conditions page. Grid cells are about 200 meters.
  • Wind: Lake Tahoe Airport observations from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive.
  • TERC keeps about one week of past model hours online. This page uses an archive saved Oct. 4, 2026, covering Sept. 20 to Oct. 6. Hours after Oct. 4 at 2 p.m. are forecast.
  • Times are Pacific. The model files carry no time zone; the daily warming cycle and the lag behind airport winds both point to local time.
  • Model surface temperatures are about 9 degrees Fahrenheit warmer than TERC's Rubicon and Homewood nearshore sensors on Oct. 4. Use temperature colors for patterns, not values.
  • The small swirls in open water come from the model at its 200 meter grid. Read the lake-wide pattern, not any single swirl.
  • Keyboard: space plays and pauses, left and right arrows step two hours.
  • Model output is provisional. It is not for navigation or swim safety decisions. Check TERC real-time conditions before going on the water.
