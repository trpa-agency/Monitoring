Noise in the Tahoe Basin: what is measured and what the record shows

TRPA monitors noise in three settings: plan areas across the Basin, the shoreline, and the highway corridors. Each has its own standard, its own sites, and its own cycle. This page gathers the three records in one place.

Plan area noise

Community noise by land use

Plan area monitoring measures the community noise equivalent level (CNEL), a 24-hour average that weights evening and night noise more heavily, at sites grouped by land use. Each land use category has its own standard, from 45 decibels in wilderness and critical wildlife habitat to 65 in industrial areas.

Shoreline

Single-event noise on the water

Shoreline monitoring records single noise events, mostly from watercraft, at sites around the lake. The standard is the number of events above the limit per day, so the measure is an exceedance rate rather than an average level.
