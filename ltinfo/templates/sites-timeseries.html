At a glance

Where and how much people walk and ride

Automated counters on shared-use paths and a few roadway locations record how many people pass by bicycle or on foot each day. The record shows seasonal use, year-over-year change, and which facilities carry the most trips.

How it is measured

Counters, counts, and coverage

What is counted

Each counter records passing bicycles and pedestrians and reports a combined daily total. Counts are directional totals, not unique people.

Equipment

Permanent infrared and inductive-loop counters (Eco-Counter) on shared-use paths, TRAFx trail counters, and short-term pneumatic tubes on roadways.

Coverage

Sites come and go as counters are installed, moved, or fail. Months with few counted days are shown with the day count so gaps are visible rather than hidden.
