## Warnings (recommend fix, not blocking)

- [Responsive] Touch targets: Calcite controls at scale `s` (the Chart/Table control, the CSV button, the site picker) are about 24 px tall, under the 44 px guideline. They match the host's control density; if the mobile audience matters, move the sites picker and the toggle to scale `m` on phones.
- [Performance] Page weight: each page loads Plotly 2.35.2 (about 3.6 MB uncompressed, 1.1 MB over the wire) from cdn.plot.ly, plus the ArcGIS SDK on the map pages. That is over the 2 MB guideline and is a documented exception for every TRPA dashboard; the plotly.js-basic bundle (about 1 MB) would cover these charts if weight becomes a problem.
- [Visual] The map canvas and the docked popup body were verified through the SDK's state and the Arcade executor, not by eye, because the browser pane was hidden for this session. The first look at the hillshade blend, the docked popup, and the popup chart color is still owed.
- [Content] Yellow cress plant counts (`COUNT_VALUE`) appear in the program dashboard popup and its chart while the plan still lists the field as unconfirmed. Not on the three pages above, but on the template they will be copied from.
